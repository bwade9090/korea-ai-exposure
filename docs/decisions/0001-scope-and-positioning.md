# ADR 0001 — Scope and positioning

- Status: accepted, 2026-10-29, with the owner's approval of `docs/PLAN.md`, which adopts these
  decisions (§1, §4).
- Decider: Hyungbae Cho

## Context

- Public AEI releases since January 2026 publish Claude.ai usage for all 17 Korean regions,
  together with use-case, collaboration, and task-trait facets. No published analysis of these
  Korean regional data was found on 2026-10-29; Phase 1.7 rechecks.
- On 15 September 2026 the Bank of Korea released an issue note on AI and regional labor markets.
  It ranks regions by generative, agentic, and physical AI exposure and tracks employment in
  high-exposure occupations, including workers in their twenties.
- Anthropic's country briefs (India, Australia) set a compact format: national profile, regional
  concentration, task mix, implications.

## Decision

1. Usage first. The headline evidence is observed regional usage. Exposure measures explain it and
   put it in context.
2. Youth employment by exposure is out of scope.
3. Public data only; descriptive analysis; no causal claims.
4. The Bank of Korea note is cited as prior art and compared at the level of published rankings.

## Consequences

- The contribution is distinct from existing exposure-based work.
- Regional measurement validity (IP geolocation, commuting) becomes the central methodological
  risk. Checkpoint B exists for it.
